Our Story

Born From Winter, Woven With Care

LASOON began with a simple belief — that everyone deserves shawls as refined as they are warm. We partner directly with weavers across Pakistan to bring you authentic, heritage-crafted pieces without the middlemen.

Every shawl tells a story: the loom it came off, the hands that wove it, the winter it's meant to soften. This isn't fast fashion. It's a keepsake.
